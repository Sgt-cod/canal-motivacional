Privacy Policy

Det Forsvunne Norge (the "App") · Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. About the App

The App is a private automation tool operated by a single individual ("I", "me"). It prepares videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned and controlled by me. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

2. Data the App accesses

When I authorize the App with my Google account, it requests the following permission only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos, set thumbnails and schedule publication on my own YouTube channels.

The App does not read my emails, contacts, files, watch history, comments or any other personal data.

3. Data storage

The only credential kept is an OAuth refresh token, stored as an encrypted secret in the private configuration of my code repository. Videos and thumbnails are generated temporarily during each run and are not retained by the App after upload. No data is stored in any database operated by me.

4. Sharing of data

I do not sell, rent, share or disclose Google user data to anyone. Data is sent only to YouTube (Google) as part of the upload itself.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token. The token can also be deleted by removing the secret from the repository settings.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
